PostFlow Documentation

How to use PostFlow to schedule and publish posts to your Facebook Pages.

Getting started

  1. Create a free PostFlow account at postflow.mandalar.app
  2. Click Connect Facebook and authorise PostFlow to access your Pages
  3. Compose a post or import multiple posts via CSV
  4. Schedule for any date and time, or publish immediately

Connecting a Facebook Page

PostFlow works with regular Facebook Pages where you are a direct Admin. Pages owned by Meta Business Suite as a primary business page are not supported due to Facebook API limitations. To use a Business Manager page, add your personal Facebook account as a direct Admin on that page first.

Scheduling posts

You can schedule posts up to 6 months in advance. PostFlow uses a server-side cron job that publishes scheduled posts at the exact minute you specify, via the official Facebook Graph API.

Bulk CSV import

Upload a CSV with columns message, scheduled_at, page_name, and media_url (optional) to schedule many posts at once.

Permissions PostFlow requests

  • pages_show_list — to list your Facebook Pages
  • pages_read_engagement — to display Page information
  • pages_read_user_content — to verify post publication
  • pages_manage_posts — to schedule and publish posts
